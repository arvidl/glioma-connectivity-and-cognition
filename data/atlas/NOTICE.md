# Atlas and template attribution

Schaefer100 cortical atlas and label table, 7-network ordering, FSLMNI152 2mm.
Downloaded from ThomasYeoLab/CBIG, fixed tag
`v0.14.3-Update_Yeo2011_Schaefer2018_labelname`, under
`stable_projects/brain_parcellation/Schaefer2018_LocalGlobal/Parcellations/MNI/`.
The original MIT license is included as CBIG_LICENSE.md.
Cite Schaefer et al., Cerebral Cortex 2018, doi:10.1093/cercor/bhx179,
and Yeo et al., J Neurophysiol 2011, doi:10.1152/jn.00338.2011.

MNI152 T1 2mm head, brain and brain mask copied from the user's installed FSL
distribution. These are the matching FSL MNI152 template, not the similarly
named MNI152NLin2009cAsym space of the supplied rsHRF derivatives.
Attribution: MNI/ICBM, McConnell Brain Imaging Centre, Montreal Neurological
Institute; FSL distribution. Template use terms:
https://fsl.fmrib.ox.ac.uk/fsl/docs/#/license
https://www.mcgill.ca/bic/software/tools-data-analysis/anatomical-mri/atlases
The original T1 lesion mask is used in native patient world coordinates;
MNI rsHRF derivatives are bundled for provenance but not mixed with this atlas.
